My Journey Learning an Impossible Song

Over the course of about three and a half years, I learned the most difficult song I've ever learned on piano. During my first semester in college,I told my friend that I could learn the song in about a month. I thought it would be a fun challenge, thinking that it was just a bit above my ability, but I soon realized I was mistaken. I'd spend a weekend and only progress a single measure, and even then, I still wouldn't be able to play it perfectly. And over the course of the next couple months, I managed to learn the first minute and a half of the song. As I listened to the song more, I realized that the difficulty of the song wasn't going to get any easier, but was probably closer to the opposite.

And so I took a break from the song and focused on learning other songs. I thought it would probably be best for me to focus on learning songs that were closer to my current level. But about a year and a half later, I decided to finish learning the rest of the chorus. I previously stopped in the middle of a section, so I thought it would be nice to at least know the rest of that part. I then took a bit of a break from learning new songs, and focused a bit more on school, while just playing songs for fun.

Then, last April, I starting learning other songs with higher difficulty, which greatly elevated my technical skills. And from there, I spent a lot of free time gradually making progress on the song. Until about a week and a half before I recorded the video of the song, I finally learned the rest of the parts. And so I've spent the time since then practicing it so I can play it all the way through. And now I just have to keep polishing the song.
